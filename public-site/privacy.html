Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or request a call back, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a call back or lab request, we collect your name, contact number, and the general reason for contact so our team can help you. We do not collect diagnosis, prescriptions, or detailed medical history through this website.

How we use it

Your details are used only to contact you and arrange care. We do not sell your information.

Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand general website usage. We never send your name, phone, email, or any medical detail to analytics tools.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, please contact the clinic.
