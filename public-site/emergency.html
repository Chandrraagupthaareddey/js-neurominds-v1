Emergency help

If you need urgent help

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or having thoughts of self-harm, please reach out for urgent help right away.

What to do now

  • Contact your nearest hospital emergency department.
  • Reach out to a trusted family member or friend who can be with you.
  • Call a local mental health helpline for immediate support.

You are not alone, and caring help is available. When you are safe, our clinic team is here to support your ongoing care.
