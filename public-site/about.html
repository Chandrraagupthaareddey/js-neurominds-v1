About us

Care rooted in compassion

JS Neurominds and Diagnostic Center is a psychiatry and mental health clinic in Chandragiri, Tirupati, offering evidence-based care for individuals and families across all ages.

Our approach

We believe mental health care should be private, respectful, and free of judgement. Every person who walks through our doors is met with warmth and a personalised plan.

What we offer

Consultations for adults, children, and seniors; online consultation; de-addiction support; counselling and psychotherapy; and convenient diagnostic services with home sample collection.
